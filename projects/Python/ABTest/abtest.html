A/B Test for Ad Effectiveness

A company has been testing two different ad compaigns. The company has been showing the B version to a subset of users through social media sites. In this project, I was required to analayse this data to answer the following questions:

  1. What were the click-through rate performances for each ad version?
  2. Did the effectiveness of ads vary across social media platforms?
  3. Should we more heavily target mobile, PC, or tablet users in future ad campaigns?

Hypothesis

Given my understanding of how the world is changing, I believe version B will be more effective as many people tend to 'doomscroll' on social media thus getting the ad more viewers. In addition, I believe there will be a difference across platforms as different demographics use different social media. Facebook for example tends to have an older demographic. Finally, I think mobile phones would be the best platform to show the ads on, as people can access their phones anywhere and thus increasing the chances of seeing the ad.
Now lets see if the data backs up my hypotheses.
